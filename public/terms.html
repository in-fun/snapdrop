What the service is

SnapDrop introduces two devices to each other so they can transfer files directly. It is free and requires no account. It is a personal project run on a best-effort basis, not a commercial product with a support contract.

What we can and cannot enforce

We should be straightforward about this. We cannot inspect what you transfer. Files move directly between devices and are encrypted by your browser; our server never receives them. There is no scanning, no filtering, no content review, and no ability to retrieve a transfer after the fact — not as a policy choice, but because the architecture gives us nothing to inspect.

This means the rules above are obligations on you rather than promises about other users. We cannot detect a violation from the content itself. What we can do is act on abuse of the service that is visible to us — connection-level abuse, attacks on the infrastructure — by blocking access. If you believe the service is being used against these terms, write to contact@snap-drop.net, though please understand what we are able to act on.

Nothing you send is stored, so nothing can be recovered. Keep your own copy of anything that matters.
